Black Buffalo Brand Ambassador and two-time UFC Heavyweight Champion Stipe Miocic returns to the octagon for the first time in over three years on Saturday, November 16 to battle current UFC Heavyweight Champion Jon Jones. Boasting a 20-4 record with 15 wins by knockout, Stipe holds the UFC record for most consecutive heavyweight title defenses as well as the record for most finishes in UFC heavyweight title fights. Though the fight was initially set to occur in November 2023, and was delayed due to Jones’s injury, Stipe has not been idle.
